CalyxRandom Privacy Policy

Who operates this app

CalyxRandom is provided by Cold Harbor of Calyx (Yangzhou) Network Technology Co., Ltd., using the developer name Cold Harbor of Calyx. This policy applies to the Android app com.calyxharbor.calyxrandom. Contact: support@calyxharbor.com.

Your lists and results

Lists, names and other text you enter, generated results, reading history, draw progress and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Random tools and bundled inspiration content work offline. We do not upload these contents to our servers or send them to advertising services. The app has no account, cloud sync, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics or remote AI service. Android cloud backup and device-to-device backup of app data are disabled by the app.

Advertising and privacy choices

CalyxRandom includes Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Advertising and consent services use the internet. Depending on your region, privacy choices and SDK behavior, Google may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app interactions, performance and diagnostic information, advertising IDs, app set IDs and other device/account identifiers. Google uses these for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. This does not include the names, lists, questions or result text you enter or generate. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Read Google's policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and controls at https://myadcenter.google.com/.

Consent and device permissions

Where required, a Google privacy message asks for your choices. A Privacy choices entry appears in Settings when UMP requires it, so you can revisit those choices. Ads are requested only when UMP reports that requests are permitted; the core tools remain usable if consent gathering or advertising fails. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise location or photo/library access. Advertising SDK network and advertising identifier permissions may appear in Android's app details. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Copying, sharing and support

Copying sends only the text you choose to the system clipboard. Sharing opens Android's share sheet; your chosen recipient or app receives the selected text under its own policies. Neither action silently uploads your full library. If you contact us by email, we receive your address and anything you include to answer your request. Avoid sending private lists or sensitive results unless necessary. We retain support correspondence only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it. My Lists lets you delete individual lists; History lets you clear saved history. These actions have different effects: deleting a list keeps its historical results, and clearing history keeps lists and settings. Android Settings > Apps > CalyxRandom > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstalling the app, removes the app's local data. Copies already shared or pasted elsewhere are controlled by those destinations. We cannot remotely restore or delete a library that was never sent to us. Google controls retention and deletion of data it receives under its policies and privacy controls.

Audience, changes and contact

CalyxRandom is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. Tarot and Words & Poems are for entertainment and reflection, not professional advice or prediction guarantees. We do not knowingly request children's personal data. Material changes to data practices will be reflected in an updated policy and, where required, an in-app notice or renewed consent. For privacy questions or deletion of support correspondence, contact support@calyxharbor.com. Effective date: 2026-10-28.

Não podemos restaurar nem excluir remotamente dados que nunca recebemos. O Google controla a retenção e a exclusão dos dados que recebe segundo suas políticas e controles.
