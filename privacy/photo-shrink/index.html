Privacy Policy — CalyxShrink

Updated November 19, 2026 · CalyxShrink (formerly Photo Shrink, com.calyxharbor.calyxshrink)

CalyxShrink compresses photos on your device. The app does not send your photos or compression history to our servers or advertising providers. If you choose Share, Android sends the selected copy to the app you choose. Any gallery backup service you have enabled is governed by that service and your settings.

1. Data We Collect and Process

1.1 Advertising identifiers (Google AdMob)

  • Google AdMob collects and shares device or advertising identifiers, IP addresses, ad interactions and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, personalization where permitted, and fraud prevention. Google can use the IP address to estimate your general location.
  • Google’s consent form appears where applicable. You can revisit the available choices in the app’s privacy settings.
  • Google's own privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy
  • You can reset or limit your advertising ID in Android Settings (Ads / Privacy).

1.2 Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

  • We use Firebase Analytics to understand feature usage (e.g., counts of compressions and size savings percentages, ad impressions). Events contain no photos and no file names — only event types (such as task_start, task_success) and technical parameters.
  • Google Analytics can associate usage events with an app-instance identifier, device information and an approximate region. CalyxShrink does not request GPS location or access your contacts.

1.3 Crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics)

  • Firebase Crashlytics receives crash reports, stack traces, installation identifiers and device or app details so we can diagnose failures. CalyxShrink does not attach photos to reports.

1.4 Remote configuration (Firebase Remote Config)

  • Firebase Remote Config fetches settings such as ad frequency. The service uses installation and app identifiers and technical information including app and operating system versions, country, language and time zone to deliver configuration values.

2. Photos and account information

  • You can use CalyxShrink without an account. It does not ask for your name, email address or phone number to compress photos.
  • You select photos through Android’s picker. The app reads the selected files and stores the compressed output and its history locally. It does not need access to your contacts, messages, microphone or camera.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Use Settings → Clear History to delete the app’s compression records and their stored copies. Uninstalling removes the app’s local data. Copies you saved in the gallery remain until you delete them there.
  • Analytics/advertising data collected by Google is governed by Google's retention policies.
